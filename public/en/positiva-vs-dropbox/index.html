The idea

Deliver better. Keep sending the same way.

Everything you do with Dropbox, you can keep doing: storing your working folders, sharing a file by link, or asking a client to upload material. But the finished work doesn’t arrive as just another file. It arrives in a branded gallery where your client can view it, choose and comment. Both from the same dashboard, with one subscription.
