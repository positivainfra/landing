FAQ

The ones that matter.

Why don't you offer unlimited storage?

Because it doesn't exist. We pay for every gigabyte we store. We'd rather tell you the number.

Do you take a commission on my albums or prints?

No. We don't have a store and we're not going to. Your client chooses in the gallery and you sell however you like.

Can I take my files with me if I leave?

Whenever you want, as a ZIP. No contract, no penalty and no need to contact support.

Does it really work for video?

Yes, and video shares the same storage as your photos. We don't charge extra for “video hours”.

Who's behind it?

Rodrigo Márquez, a photographer and filmmaker from Valencia, Spain, who is also a software engineer. After twelve years looking for a tool like this, he built it.
