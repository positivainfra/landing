FAQ

The ones that matter.

Can I start without paying?

Yes. The Free plan is there so you can try the whole workflow on real jobs, and you move to a paid plan when the volume calls for it. See the plans in detail.

Does video use separate storage?

No. Photos and video share the same storage on your plan. There are no “video hours” and no separate product to buy.

Does it replace my file transfer tool?

For client work, yes: links never expire, carry your brand and control what can be downloaded. You can also share a single file without setting up a gallery. Your brand: Author and Studio plans

What about a portfolio website?

Included on every plan: four pages with your name on your subdomain, built from work you’ve already uploaded. How it works.

What happens in a month with no jobs?

Nothing: your galleries stay where they are and the links you’ve delivered keep working. Your archive doesn’t depend on your month.
