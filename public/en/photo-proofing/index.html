“I love 231, 247 and the one with the fisherman.” Not anymore.

Online client proofing for photographers: favorites and comments, photo by photo

Your client marks favorites and comments on each photo right in the gallery. The selection shows up in your dashboard instantly, and you export it to your editor. No screenshots, no lists typed out by hand.

How it works

The selection happens inside the gallery.

Nothing to install and nothing to explain: your client opens their link, goes through the photos and picks. Everything they mark and comment on stays on the exact photo, not in a text thread.

01

One tap to mark

A heart on every photo. The selection grows as your client goes through the gallery, on their phone or their computer.

02

On the exact photo

“This one, but in black and white” is written on that photo, not in a separate email. No context to guess at.

03

The selection, in your editor

Bring the favorites into Lightroom Classic with the Positiva plugin, or export the file names as a .txt list, a .csv spreadsheet or Finder color tags.

Your dashboard

You see it come in, organized.

Every favorite and every comment appears in your dashboard as it happens. And when several people are reviewing, you decide whether comments are shared or individual.

  • All in your dashboard. The selection and the comments, together and up to date. No more piecing feedback together from different places. Mark each comment resolved and what's left open is what's still to do.
  • Collaborative or Individual. In a proofing gallery, every reviewer signs in with a verified email. Everyone sees everyone's comments, or each person only sees their own: you set it per gallery.
  • Compare versions. Replace a photo with a corrected version and your client can drag a slider between the previous one and the current one to see exactly what changed.
  • From proofing to delivery. The same gallery becomes the delivery once the selection is final. Nothing to upload again.
FAQ

The ones that matter.

Does my client need to create an account?

To view the gallery and mark favorites, the link is all they need: no accounts, nothing to install. In proofing galleries, each reviewer signs in with a verified email, so every comment has an author.

How do I get the selection?

In your dashboard, as it happens. Then you export it to apply in your editor: as a Lightroom Classic collection with the Positiva plugin, a list of file names (.txt), a spreadsheet (.csv) or Finder color tags.

Can several people review at the same time?

Yes. And you choose the mode per gallery: Collaborative, where everyone sees everyone's comments, or Individual, where each person only sees their own.

Is it on the Free plan?

Yes: favorites and photo comments are included from the Free plan. See the plans in detail.

What about video?

Video review pins every comment to its frame and exports markers to DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro. Video review in depth. Author and Studio plans
