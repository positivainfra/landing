FAQ

The ones that matter.

Do I have to upload my photos again for the portfolio?

No. The portfolio is built from the galleries you’ve already uploaded: as soon as a piece is in Positiva, it’s available for your website.

Which plans include the portfolio website?

All of them, including Free. Video on your portfolio website — uploaded or linked — is available on the Author and Studio plans.

Can I turn my portfolio website off?

Yes, with the “Turn off profile” switch. Nothing is deleted: your content stays exactly as it is, and anyone who opens your address is redirected to positiva.studio until you turn it back on. Your private galleries keep working with their links.

What’s the difference between the portfolio and a public gallery?

Adding a piece to the portfolio shows that photo or video on your website. Making a gallery public shows the whole gallery, with everything in it. They’re two separate decisions.

Can I use my own domain?

Your website is live at yourstudio.positiva.studio from day one, on every plan. On the Studio plan you can serve it from your own custom domain. How your brand works in Positiva.
