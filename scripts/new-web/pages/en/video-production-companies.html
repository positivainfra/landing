FAQ

The ones that matter.

How many people can review the same project?

As many as you need: each reviewer signs in with a verified email. In Collaborative mode everyone sees everyone’s comments; in Individual mode, each person only sees their own.

Does the end client see Positiva’s branding?

With white label — from the Author plan — the gallery carries your logo and colors, and on the Studio plan, your own domain. How your branding works.

How do video changes get to the editor?

Comments are exported as markers for DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro: they show up on the timeline, on their frame, with each reviewer’s text. Author and Studio plans

Can I cut off access when the project ends?

Yes. Each link can be revoked on its own without touching the others, and you can create new links with different permissions at any time.

Does it work for delivering the master too?

Yes: the same review gallery becomes the delivery without losing anything, or you share the master as a single file with its own link. Delivery in depth.
