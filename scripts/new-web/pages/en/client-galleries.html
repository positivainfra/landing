FAQ

The ones that matter.

Does the gallery link expire?

No. Links have no expiry date, and they don’t change when you replace files in the gallery.

Does my client need to create an account?

No. They open the link in any browser, with no account and nothing to install. For a delivery, access is controlled by the link’s password.

Can I give the couple and the guests different permissions?

Yes. One gallery can have as many links as you need, each with its own password and download quality: view only, web, or high with originals.

Can I deliver photos and video in the same gallery?

Yes. Photos and video share the same gallery, the same moments and the same link, and video plays inside the gallery. Storage is a single pool for both.

Which file formats does a gallery support?

JPEG for photos and MP4 with H.264 for video — the formats a web browser can play. RAW files aren’t supported.

Can I see whether my client has opened the gallery?

Yes. Every gallery tracks visits, unique visitors, downloads and plays. Author and Studio plans

What happens if I replace a file after delivery?

The link stays the same and the previous version is kept. Nobody has to download anything again or ask for a new link.
