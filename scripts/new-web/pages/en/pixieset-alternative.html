Before you switch

Why people leave Pixieset (and why many stay).

Pixieset is four products: Client Gallery, Website, Store and Studio Manager. The usual gallery plan, Plus, costs $16/month billed annually ($192/year) for 100 GB. These are the reasons that come up when photographers look for a Pixieset alternative:

  • Video is an add-on to a photo product. Basic includes 30 minutes of HD video; on higher plans video counts against your storage, and 4K is “available on select upgraded” plans. We couldn’t find timecoded comments on video in their help center.
  • The suite is sold in pieces. Galleries, website, store and Studio Manager are separate subscriptions, or the Suite from $28/month billed annually (100 GB of photos and 1 hour of video).
  • Proofing stops at favorites. Clients build favorite lists and can add notes to favorited photos, which you can take to Lightroom. That covers photo selection; it doesn’t cover feedback on a cut.
  • The Free plan takes a cut. 3 GB, and store sales carry a 15% commission (0% on paid plans).
  • Where your files live. Pixieset’s data processing agreement allows transfers outside the EEA and doesn’t commit to EU storage. That matters if you or your clients are in Europe.

And the reason to stay: if you already run your website, store and contracts inside Pixieset, no alternative here gives you those four things back under one login.

Best for: photographers and videographers who deliver both and need client feedback

Positiva

What it does well

  • Photos and video share one gallery, one link and one storage quota: 250 GB on Author, 1 TB on Studio. No minutes, no separate video quota.
  • Favorites and comments on every photo on all plans, Free included (photo proofing). Favorites go to Lightroom Classic with a plugin.
  • Frame-accurate comments on video (video review), exported as markers: EDL for DaVinci Resolve, FCPXML for Final Cut Pro. Author and Studio plans
  • Links that never expire on paid plans, a portfolio website built from your deliveries, EU hosting, and your work never trains AI.
  • Files with folders, multi-file transfers and file requests, so clients or vendors upload to you without an account (up to 150 GB per file). Studio plan

Limitations

  • No print store. You can’t sell prints, albums or products from a gallery.
  • No contracts, invoices, booking or CRM. There is no equivalent of Studio Manager.
  • No native DaVinci Resolve or Premiere plugin: video comments leave as a file (EDL or FCPXML) that you import.
  • Galleries play browser formats (H.264, HEVC). Editing codecs such as ProRes are not played: upload a delivery export.
  • The Free plan allows 2 active galleries that expire after 30 days, without your branding; video comments need a paid plan.
  • Storage is per plan (250 GB or 1 TB, plus add-ons). There is no unlimited tier.
  • Our help center is in Spanish only for now.

Price: Free, €0 (15 GB). Author, €89/year excl. VAT (or €8.95/month), 250 GB. Studio, €219/year excl. VAT (or €22.95/month), 1 TB, with custom domain, multi-brand, proofing rounds and file requests. Extra storage: +100 GB for €19/year.

We wouldn’t pick it if you sell prints from your galleries or want contracts and invoices in the same tool.

Best for: photographers whose print and album sales justify the tool

Pic-Time

What it does well

  • Print store and sales automations listed on every plan.
  • Video has its own quota, with add-ons up to 5 TB.
  • Professional removes Pic-Time branding and adds a custom domain and client selections.

Limitations

  • Client selections and album proofing start at Professional; Free and Beginner don’t include them.
  • Video is a separate quota: 30 GB on Professional (about 3 hours). Add-ons start at $10/month billed annually for 100 GB.
  • The Free plan drops from 10 GB to 3 GB after 3 months, with 1 GB of video.
  • Images are indexed for AI search unless you opt out, and their AI FAQ says the model may train on a small share of images from photographers with AI search enabled.

Price: Beginner, $7/month billed annually ($8 monthly). Professional, $21/month billed annually ($252/year) or $25 monthly. Advanced, $42/month billed annually or $50 monthly.

Best for: photo studios that want galleries and paperwork in one place

ShootProof

What it does well

  • Contracts and invoices included on every paid plan.
  • ShootProof says it never takes a commission on sales.
  • Video up to 4K on paid plans.

Limitations

  • The Free plan is 3 GB, photos only.
  • On Starter and Standard, video shares the photo storage (20 GB or 100 GB); Professional adds a separate 50 GB for video.
  • Getting favorites into Lightroom means copying filenames into a Lightroom filter, per their help center.
  • We couldn't find comments on video in their documentation.

Price: Starter, $8.33/month billed annually ($9.99 monthly), 20 GB. Standard, $16.67/month billed annually ($19.99 monthly), 100 GB. Professional, $26.67/month billed annually, 1,000 GB + 50 GB of video. Premium, $50/month billed annually.

Best for: US photographers who want a CRM and galleries at a low price

CloudSpot

What it does well

  • Studio Manager (contracts, invoicing, client management) included from the Lite plan.
  • Commission-free store from Lite.
  • Clients can keep several favorites lists per gallery.

Limitations

  • Video is added as links or Vimeo/YouTube embeds, according to their pricing page; we couldn’t find hosted video upload.
  • Invoicing and online payments are US-only.
  • The Free plan is 5 GB, 3 galleries, JPEG only, with a 15% store commission.

Price: Entry, $7/month or $72/year (15 GB). Lite, $17/month or $144/year (100 GB). Pro, $34/month (500 GB). Unlimited, $50/month. Annual prices for Pro and Unlimited are not published in their help center.

Best for: one subscription for website, galleries and store

Zenfolio

What it does well

  • A website on every plan.
  • Store from Professional.
  • Advanced: unlimited JPEG storage plus 150 GB of video, in 4K.

Limitations

  • No free plan, only a trial.
  • Basic is 15 GB with HD video and no store.
  • Booking is a $10/month add-on below Advanced.
  • We couldn't find frame-level video review in their documentation.

Price: Basic, $7/month billed annually ($9 monthly), 15 GB. Professional, $11.50/month billed annually ($23 monthly), 150 GB. Advanced, $20/month billed annually ($40 monthly). Their page shows a first-year offer until October 31, 2026.

Best for: large photo archives with a portfolio site

SmugMug

What it does well

  • Unlimited photo uploads on every plan.
  • Prints and photo gifts can be ordered on any account level.
  • Plans scale from a simple photo home to selling.

Limitations

  • Client tools are on the top plan only ($41.99/month).
  • No free plan: a 14-day trial.
  • Video limits are not stated on their plans page.

Price: $7, $11, $27 and $41.99/month billed monthly; billing annually saves between 29% and 38%, depending on the plan.

How to decide

Match the tool to the job.

If prints pay your bills, Pic-Time is the natural replacement for Pixieset’s galleries. If what ties you to Pixieset is Studio Manager, ShootProof includes contracts and invoices, and CloudSpot does it for less if you’re in the US. If you archive tens of thousands of photos, SmugMug. If you want to stop paying for separate modules, Zenfolio.

And Positiva is our pick if you deliver photos and video, need your client to give feedback on both, and don’t sell prints. Compare it with the other big one on our Pic-Time alternative page, or try it free with 15 GB before moving anything.

FAQ

The questions that matter.

What is the closest alternative to Pixieset?

For galleries, Pic-Time: the same kind of client gallery with a print store and sales automations. To replace the whole suite (galleries, website, store and studio management) you would need Zenfolio plus a CRM, or ShootProof or CloudSpot for the paperwork side.

Which Pixieset alternative is best for video?

Positiva treats video like photos: same gallery, same storage, and clients comment on the exact frame, with comments exported as markers for DaVinci Resolve (EDL) and Final Cut Pro (FCPXML). Picflow also offers frame-accurate annotations on video. Pixieset’s Basic plan includes 30 minutes of HD video and offers 4K only on select plans.

Is there a free Pixieset alternative?

Several have permanent free plans: Positiva (15 GB, 2 galleries that expire after 30 days), Pic-Time (10 GB, reduced to 3 GB after 3 months), ShootProof (3 GB, photos only), CloudSpot (5 GB, 3 galleries) and Picflow (2 GB). Zenfolio and SmugMug offer trials only. Pixieset’s own free plan is 3 GB with a 15% store commission.

Does Positiva have a print store or contracts like Pixieset?

No. Positiva has no print store and no contracts, invoices or booking. It focuses on delivering and reviewing photos and video with your branding. If you need those, Pic-Time, ShootProof or Pixieset itself fit better.

Can I move my galleries from Pixieset?

We couldn’t find an official automatic migration between any of these platforms. The usual path is to re-upload your originals from your own archive; new galleries won’t keep favorites or order history.

Where is my data stored?

Positiva stores your files in the EU. Pixieset’s data processing agreement allows transfers outside the EEA without committing to EU storage. Pic-Time’s GDPR article doesn’t state a storage region.
